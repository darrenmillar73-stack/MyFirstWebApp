# **GREEK STIFADO** 

## Dutch Oven • Serves 4–6

Rich, slow-braised Greek beef with whole shallots, red wine, vinegar and warming spices.



### **🧺 INGREDIENTS**

#### Beef \& Onions

1.2–1.5kg beef chuck or braising steak, cut into large chunks

700–900g small shallots, peeled and left whole

3 garlic cloves, finely chopped

2 tbsp olive oil

Salt

Freshly ground black pepper



#### **Sauce**

2 tbsp tomato purée

400g tin chopped tomatoes

300ml dry red wine

50ml red wine vinegar

200–250ml beef stock

Herbs \& Spices

1 cinnamon stick

2 whole cloves

½ tsp ground allspice

2 bay leaves

1 tsp dried oregano



### **👨‍🍳 METHOD**

1\. Heat the oven

&#x20;Preheat to 160°C / 140°C fan.

2\. Brown the beef

&#x20;Pat the beef dry and season generously with salt and black pepper.

Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in the Dutch oven over medium-high heat.

Brown the beef in batches until deeply coloured on all sides. Don't crowd the pan.

Remove and set aside.

3\. Brown the shallots

&#x20;Add the remaining olive oil.

Add the whole shallots and cook for 5–7 minutes, turning occasionally, until lightly golden.

Remove and set aside with the beef.

4\. Build the sauce

&#x20;Reduce to medium heat.

Add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds.

Add the tomato purée and cook for 1–2 minutes.

Add:

½ tsp ground allspice

Cinnamon stick

Whole cloves

Bay leaves

Oregano

Stir for about 30 seconds.

5\. Deglaze

&#x20;Pour in the red wine and red wine vinegar.

Scrape all the browned bits from the bottom of the Dutch oven.

Simmer for 4–5 minutes.

6\. Assemble

&#x20;Add the chopped tomatoes.

Return the beef and any resting juices to the Dutch oven.

Add enough beef stock to bring the liquid roughly ¾ of the way up the meat.

Bring gently to a simmer.

7\. Slow cook

&#x20;Cover with the lid and place in the oven for 2 hours.

Add the whole shallots, gently pushing them into the sauce.

Cover again and cook for another 45–60 minutes, until the beef is extremely tender and the shallots are soft but still mostly intact.

8\. Thicken

&#x20;Remove the lid for the final 20–30 minutes if the sauce needs reducing.

The finished sauce should be thick, dark and glossy, rather than watery.

9\. Finish

&#x20;Remove the cinnamon stick, bay leaves and any cloves you can find.

Taste and adjust with:

Salt

Black pepper

A small splash of red wine vinegar if it needs more brightness

Leave to rest for 10–15 minutes before serving.



### **🍽️ SERVE WITH**

🥔 Creamy mashed potatoes

&#x20;🥔 Greek-style roast potatoes

&#x20;🍞 Crusty bread

&#x20;🥗 Simple Greek salad



#### **⭐ MAKE-AHEAD TIP**

Stifado is even better the following day.

Cool completely, refrigerate overnight, then gently reheat in the Dutch oven. The beef absorbs even more of the wine, onion and spice flavours.



#### **🔥 KEY TO A GREAT STIFADO**

Brown the beef properly + keep the shallots whole + don't rush the braise.

You want fall-apart beef, sweet tender shallots and a rich wine-and-vinegar sauce with cinnamon and clove in the background.
