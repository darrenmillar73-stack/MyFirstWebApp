🍕 NEAPOLITAN CHICKEN & MUSHROOM (PIZZERIA PERFECT – 450°C)

🧱 DOUGH (1 pizza – 10–11")
• 200 g 00 flour
• 140 ml cold water (≈70% hydration)
• ¾ tsp fine salt
• ¼ tsp fast-action yeast
BREAD MAKER (DMD)
Water
Flour
Salt (one side)
Yeast (opposite side)
Run DOUGH program
After:
• Rest 10 minutes
• Form tight dough ball
• Lightly oil container (NOT flour)
• Refrigerate 24 hours (best)
• Remove 1–2 hours before baking

🍅 NEAPOLITAN SAUCE (AUTHENTIC)
• 200 g chopped tomatoes (Cirio/Napolina)
• ¼–½ tsp fine salt
• Optional: ½ tsp olive oil
Method:
• Hand crush only (no blender)
• Keep slightly chunky
• Do NOT cook
Use:
• ~2 tbsp (≈50 g)
• Thin, light layer

🧀 CHEESE
• 70–80 g fresh mozzarella
Prep:
• Tear into small pieces
• Dry on kitchen paper 20–30 mins

🍗🍄 TOPPINGS
• 50–60 g cooked chicken (SHREDDED)
• 25–30 g mushrooms (thin sliced)
Prep:
• Mushrooms → dry sauté 2–3 mins (remove moisture)
• Season chicken lightly:
pinch salt
pinch black pepper
Optional after bake:
• 1–2 tsp green pesto
• fresh basil
• light parmesan

🏗️ BUILD
• Light semolina dust on peel
• Stretch dough to 10–11" (leave 1.5–2 cm rim)
• Add ~2 tbsp sauce (thin layer)
• Add mozzarella evenly (not clumped)
• Add shredded chicken (light, even)
• Add mushrooms (sparse layer)

🔥 BAKE (Tower Pizza Oven)
• Preheat 15–20 mins
• Set to 450°C
• Bake 2.5–4 minutes total
• Turn every 20–30 seconds after first minute

✨ FINISH
• Drizzle pesto (light, not spread)
• Add fresh basil (torn)
• Optional light parmesan
• Light drizzle olive oil

⚠️ KEY TIPS
• Dry mozzarella well (prevents soggy centre)
• ALWAYS pre-cook mushrooms
• Keep toppings light (Neapolitan = minimal)
• Watch pizza, not the clock
• Turn frequently

🔥 RESULT
Airy, puffed crust with light char
Soft but not wet centre
Balanced toppings
Clean tomato flavour
True Neapolitan-style pizza at home
