🍛 Easy Chicken Curry (Dutch Oven)

Serves 3–4
🧺 Ingredients
600g diced chicken breast (or thighs)
2 medium onions, diced
1 green chilli, deseeded & diced
4 garlic cloves, crushed
2cm piece ginger, grated
400g chopped tomatoes
200ml boiling water
1 chicken stock cube
2½ tbsp tomato purée
1 tbsp garam masala
2 tsp ground cumin
2 tsp ground coriander
1 tsp turmeric
1 tsp mild chilli powder
1 tsp white granulated sweetener
1 tsp cider vinegar
10g fresh coriander, chopped VB
Low-calorie cooking spray

👨‍🍳 Method
Spray the Dutch oven with cooking spray and heat over medium heat.
Fry the onions for 8–10 minutes until softened.
Add the garlic, chilli and ginger. Cook for 2–3 minutes.
Stir in all the spices and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
Add the chicken and cook for 4–5 minutes, stirring until lightly browned.
Dissolve the stock cube in 200ml boiling water, then stir in the tomato purée, sweetener and cider vinegar.
Pour the stock mixture and chopped tomatoes into the Dutch oven and stir well.
Bring to a gentle simmer.
Reduce the heat to low, cover with the lid slightly ajar and simmer for 20–25 minutes, stirring occasionally.
Stir through the fresh coriander before serving.

🍽️ Serve With
Basmati rice
Naan bread
Greek yoghurt (optional)
💡 Tip: Your enamelled Dutch oven retains heat well, so once simmering, the lowest hob setting is usually enough.
